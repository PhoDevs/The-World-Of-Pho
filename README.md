# The World of Pho

A mobile-first strategic board-battle and idle town-economy game.

Combine turn-based tactical grid battles against AI or online opponents with a rich incremental town—featuring farming, mining, gem crafting, horse racing, guilds, and a player-driven online marketplace.

## Features

### Strategic Board Battles

- **Turn-Based Grid Combat**: Draft pieces from your inventory, place them on the 2D board, maneuver across dynamic terrain, and score territory influence.
- **Environmental Mechanics**: Battle across varied terrains including indoors, outdoors, ice (sliding), wind (pushing tiles), and mud.
- **AI Opponents**: Test your strategy against four distinct AI personalities—**Random** (Easy), **NPC** (Medium), **Wolf** (Hard), and **Bear** (Brutal).
- **Online Multiplayer**: Real-time matchmaking, lobbies, and synchronized board battles over WebSockets.

### Town & Idle Economies

- **Garden & Orchard**: Plant and water crops, grow fruit trees, and defend your harvest in the **Hare Chase** minigame.
- **Mining, Deep Mine & Gold Panning**: Dig through multi-layer mines with excavator upgrades, or shake and sift for gold and gemstones.
- **Workshop**: Cut raw gems and forge rings, necklaces, and charms for permanent stat bonuses.
- **Business District**: Unlock and upgrade idle shops that generate revenue while you're away.
- **Horse Racing**: Breed and train horses, compete in sprint and hurdle minigames, and place bets at the racetrack.
- **Guilds**: Join cooperative guilds with shared gardens, mines, stockpiles, production, leaderboards, and a guild store.
- **Online Marketplace**: Trade items with other players, track price history charts, and sync accounts.
- **Progression**: Level up, unlock achievements, complete missions, and prestige.
